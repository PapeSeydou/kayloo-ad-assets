## Brand color

`#005AAB` is the solid ink color in the official 2026 logo, read from opaque pixels in `haier-logo-2026.png`. The white version is `#FFFFFF`.

No published brand color palette was found on the Haier pages checked. Treat this as a logo-derived hex, not a published brand guideline.
